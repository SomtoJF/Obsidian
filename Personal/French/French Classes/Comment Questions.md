==Comment means how==
- When you meet someone new you ask ==comment== questions. These are typically questions asking ==how things are going==. Therefore, for comment questions, the most important verb to completely understand is Aller (To Go).

## Conjugation of Aller
**Aller is an irregular verb** so it can't be conjugated using the normal formula for ER verbs.

### Present Tense (Présent)

- **Je** vais (I go / am going)
- **Tu** vas (You go)
- **Il / Elle / On** va (He / She / One goes)
- **Nous** allons (We go)
- **Vous** allez (You go)
- **Ils / Elles** vont (They go) [[1](https://www.mathildekien.com/podcast-episodes/23), [2](https://theperfectfrench.com/french-word-of-the-day/aller/), [3](https://conjugator.reverso.net/conjugation-french-verb-aller.html)]

### Passé Composé (Compound Past)

_Conjugated with **être** + the past participle **allé(e)(s)**._ [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PpHYafvH-5c&t=8), [2](https://www.lawlessfrench.com/verb-conjugations/aller/)]

- **Je suis** allé(e) (I went)
- **Tu es** allé(e) (You went)
- **Il / Elle / On est** allé(e) (He / She / One went)
- **Nous sommes** allé(e)s (We went)
- **Vous êtes** allé(e)(s) (You went)
- **Ils / Elles sont** allé(e)s (They went) [[1](https://www.thoughtco.com/french-expressions-with-aller-1368643), [2](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Mh2A0P7I9lE), [3](https://www.mathildekien.com/podcast-episodes/23), [4](https://www.lawlessfrench.com/verb-conjugations/aller/), [5](https://theperfectfrench.com/french-word-of-the-day/aller/)]

### Future Simple (Futur Simple)

_Uses the irregular stem **ir-**._ [[1](https://leconjugueur.lefigaro.fr/french/verb/aller.html)]

- **J'irai** (I will go)
- **Tu iras** (You will go)
- **Il / Elle / On ira** (He / She / One will go)
- **Nous irons** (We will go)
- **Vous irez** (You will go)
- **Ils / Elles iront** (They will go) [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cgf69doEZ6g&t=13), [2](https://french.kwiziq.com/revision/grammar/conjugate-aller-in-le-futur-future-tense)]

### Imperfect (Imparfait)

_Uses the regular stem **all-**._ [[1](https://leconjugueur.lefigaro.fr/french/verb/aller.html)]

- **J'allais** (I was going)
- **Tu allais** (You were going)
- **Il / Elle / On allait** (He / She / One was going)
- **Nous allions** (We were going)
- **Vous alliez** (You were going)
- **Ils / Elles allaient** (They were going)
