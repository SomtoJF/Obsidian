==All French nouns, unlike English nouns, have a gender==. Every noun is either masculine or feminine. Nouns that refer specifically to males (people or animals), such as father, son, etc., are masculine. Those that refer to females (people or animals), such as mother, daughter, etc., are feminine. 
For most other nouns, gender is usually arbitrary and must be memorized.
The definite article (the) that accompanies masculine nouns is *le*. *La* accompanies feminine nouns. *L’* is used before masculine or feminine nouns beginning with a vowel or silent h.
