A modal verb is ==a helping verb used with a main verb to show **ability**, **possibility**, **permission**, or **obligation**==.
The major modal verbs we have are
- Pouvoir - Can
- Devoir - To have to (must)
- Savoir - To know
- Vouloir - To want
- Falloir - To be necessary/must
- Aimer - To Like
==Falloir is an impersonal verb==, it can only be conjugated with **il**. The **Il means it**
