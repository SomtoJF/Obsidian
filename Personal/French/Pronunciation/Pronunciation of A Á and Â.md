## A and À
$A$ is pronounced sharply. Like "cat" same as $À$ 
**Examples**
**At the start of the word**
- Un Achat - A purchase
- Avoir - To have
- Aller - To go
**In the middle of the word**
- Par - By
- Une marre - A pond
- Une tasse - A cup
- Un tache - A stain
**At the end of the word**
- Sa - His/Her/Its
- Fera - Will do
- Voilà - Here
- Là - There
- Déjà - Already

## Â
$Â$ is pronounced softly.
**Examples**
- Une Tâche - A task
- Un lâche - A coward
- Avoir hâte - Can't wait
