## UN and UM
Adding E activates the `U (oo)` sound

| Word       | Sound      | Meaning     | Altered Word | Sound           | Meaning    |
| ---------- | ---------- | ----------- | ------------ | --------------- | ---------- |
| Le Parfum  | `/par-fa/` | The Perfume | Perfumer     | `/par-foo-may/` | To perfume |
| Un (m)     | `/a/`      | A           | Une (f)      | `/oon/`         | A          |
| Brun (m)   | `/bra/`    | Brown       | Brune (f)    | `/broon/`       | Brown      |
| Chacun (m) | /sha-ca/   | Each        | Chacune (f)  | /sha-coon/      | Each       |
|            |            |             |              |                 |            |
## IN, EIN and AIN

| Word        | Sound      | Meaning  | Altered Word | Sound         | Meaning   |
| ----------- | ---------- | -------- | ------------ | ------------- | --------- |
| Un jardin   | `/jar-da/` | A garden | Jardiner     | `/ja-di-nay/` | To garden |
| Fin (m)     | `/fa/`     | Thin     | Fine (f)     | `/fin/`       | Thin      |
| Un frein    | `/fra/`    | A brake  | Friener      | `/fre-nay/`   | To brake  |
| Certain (m) | `/ser-ta/` | certain  | Certaine (f) | `/ser-ten/`   | certain   |
